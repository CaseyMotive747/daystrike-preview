What we collect

When you join the DAYSTRIKE waitlist, we collect your email address and, if you choose to share them, your first name and flavor preference. We also ask you to confirm that you are 18 or older.

Age

DAYSTRIKE contains caffeine and is intended for adults 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes
