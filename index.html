FAQ

Questions we get

Is this a recovery drink?

No. It's a daily energy and hydration drink for people with full days.

When should I drink it?

Mornings or before a workout, in 12 to 16 oz of water over ice. Finish your last serving by early afternoon.

How much caffeine is in a serving?

180 mg, about two cups of coffee.

Can I have more than one a day?

Most healthy adults can have up to about 400 mg of caffeine a day, according to the FDA. Count your coffee and other caffeine too. We recommend finishing your last serving by early afternoon so it doesn't get in the way of sleep. Don't exceed two servings a day.
