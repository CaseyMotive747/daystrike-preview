Who DAYSTRIKE isn't for

Anyone under 18, pregnant or nursing, or sensitive to caffeine. If you have a medical condition or take medication, check with your doctor first. Not intended to be mixed with alcohol.

FAQ

Good questions.

Is this a hangover cure?

No, and we won't pretend it is. DAYSTRIKE is an energy and hydration drink. It doesn't undo a late night. Drink water, get some sleep, and never drive or train while impaired.

How much caffeine is in a serving?

150 mg, about what's in a strong cup of coffee. It's printed clearly on every stick and tub.

Can I have more than one a day?

Most healthy adults can have up to about 400 mg of caffeine a day, according to the FDA. Count your coffee and other caffeine too. We recommend finishing your last serving by early afternoon so it doesn't get in the way of sleep.

Where is it made?

In the U.S., with manufacturing partners that hold third-party quality certifications.
